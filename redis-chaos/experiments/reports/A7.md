# A7. Manual `CLUSTER FAILOVER` to replica dc2-s1

Run: `experiments/runs/A7-20261020-170220`. A coordinated failover: the
master stops serving, waits for the replica to catch up, hands over.

## Result

0 errors, 14016 ok, max latency 5.3ms, 0 lost writes.

## Client

One `beginning slot refresh` / `slot refresh complete` pair at 5.0s and
nothing else: the old master answered the first write after the handover
with MOVED, the request was redirected and succeeded within its retry
budget, and the refresh ran once. The per-second lines show a 2.6ms p99 at
6s and nothing more.

## Reading

This is the path redis-rs handles well: a reachable node that knows the new
owner tells the client where to go. Everything that goes wrong in the other
experiments is about the cases where nobody reachable says MOVED.
